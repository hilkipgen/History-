# Class XII History, Unit XIII: Gandhi and the Indian Nationalist Movement

**File:** `Class_XII_Unit_XIII_Gandhi_Animated_Lecture.pptx` (34 slides, 16:9, about 5 MB)

This is an animated, documentary-style redesign of the original 34-slide, 90-minute lecture deck. The slide order, the time guide on every slide and all of the original text are kept. Every sentence of the original deck appears on its slide, word for word. The two exceptions are layout splits: on slide 14 the Bardoli bullet is shown as two sentences in a cause-and-effect chain, and on slide 16 the "eleven demands" sentence is shown as a lead line plus five animated demand chips. That full sentence is also kept in the speaker notes.

## How to present

- Open the file in Microsoft PowerPoint and present normally. Each click (→, Space or the clicker) reveals the next item. Nothing advances on its own.
- **Slide 1** plays its title reveal automatically when it opens (about 6 s). Slides 4, 6, 8, 18, 25 and 34 bring in their opening photograph automatically.
- **Speaker notes** on every slide give the time guide and what each click shows. Image credits are in the notes of slide 34.
- All text is in normal editable text boxes. Photographs, maps, icons and diagrams are separate objects, so they can be moved or deleted.

## Visual design

- Parchment and cream content slides with dark navy title and closing slides, and muted saffron accents. Fonts are Cambria for headings and Calibri for body text.
- Each slide has a section label, a time-guide chip and a footer. Teacher prompts appear in a navy box, revealed last.
- Key terms (Satyagraha, Ahimsa, Swaraj, Purna Swaraj, Quit India, Do or Die, Khudai Khidmatgars) are highlighted in saffron. Dates are in bold.
- Every image is labelled **ARCHIVAL PHOTOGRAPH**, **PRESENT-DAY PHOTOGRAPH**, **ILLUSTRATIVE MAP**, **ILLUSTRATION** or **DIAGRAM**, so reconstructions are never presented as archival records.

## Maps

The base maps are drawn from Natural Earth data (public domain), using India's official boundary view. The subcontinent is shown without internal borders, which suits the pre-1947 period. Pins, labels and routes are separate PowerPoint shapes. Routes and locations are **approximate and illustrative**, and each map is labelled that way.

## Notes and limitations

- The animations were written directly as native PowerPoint animation XML. The file passes Office Open XML schema validation, and LibreOffice imports all of the effects. I could not open it in Microsoft PowerPoint itself, so please run through it once in Slide Show before the lecture.
- Slide previews were checked in LibreOffice, which substitutes metric-compatible fonts for Cambria and Calibri, so PowerPoint should lay out the text the same way.
- I planned an archival film still of the 1942 Quit India footage for slide 26, but Wikimedia could not supply a frame. That slide uses an animated map instead.
- Wikimedia Commons dates the Dandi salt photograph to 5 April 1930, while the chapter says the Salt Law was broken on 6 April. The caption therefore reads "April 1930", so it does not contradict the chapter.
